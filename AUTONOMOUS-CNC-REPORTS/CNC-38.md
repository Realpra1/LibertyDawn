# CNC-38 — Early VIKI infantry rush

## Current task specification

During a bounded early-game window, ordinary VIKI must reserve and send chemical warriors (`e5`) as independent harassment missions against visible reachable enemy harvesters, refineries, and power structures. Chemical warriors remain individual actors with distinct targets where possible; they must not be merged into the grenadier groups or ordinary ground squads while their missions are active.

VIKI must also assemble at most two independent groups of exactly ten grenadiers (`e2`) and rush enemy bases. Each group must keep deterministic spacing rather than converging on one cell. Grenadiers should fight structures from useful range, withdraw or reform away from nearby enemy units, and pause forward retargeting after destroying a target so the formation does not walk through its own in-flight or impact grenades. The behavior must release actors after the early window, branch/buildability loss, ownership/death changes, transport/protection/repair contention, or mission completion, and its bounded production demand must not duplicate queued/live units or permanently monopolize infantry queues.

The literal player-visible scenario grants a covert VIKI at least twenty grenadiers and several chemical warriors against an enemy base with exposed economy targets and nearby defenders. The chemical warriors visibly depart one by one for distinct economy targets. Two separately logged ten-grenadier groups approach on spaced cells, attack from range, delay their next advance after a kill, and reform away from an enemy unit that enters their safety radius without grenadier splash causing avoidable friendly deaths.

Forbidden outcomes include a third grenadier group; partial groups launching before ten members; chemical warriors moving as one blob; ordinary squads, exploration, APC/helicopter transport, protection, capture, or another specialist stealing reserved infantry; requesting unbuildable infantry forever; duplicate production demand; draining emergency economy recovery after the early window; selecting hidden, allied, dead, unreachable, or unattackable targets; bunching grenadiers onto one destination; immediately advancing through a killed target's impact area; closing into enemy units when a safer reachable firing formation exists; unbounded scans/path searches; nondeterministic ties; save/load corruption; or release debug spam.

## Contention inventory

The shared infantry queues are also driven by VIKI's adaptive `UnitBuilderBotModule`, the twelve-rifle/eight-rocket opening garrison and its emergency burst, and player queue state. Live `e2`/`e5` actors may be consumed or ordered by the ordinary rush/general/protection squads, normal or emergency crate exploration, APC/Chinook assault and rescue transport, repair/resupply activities, and any other `IBotUnitReservations` provider. Actor death, capture, cargo state, a target disappearing into shroud, unreachable terrain, branch loss, save/load, and the early-window boundary can all invalidate a mission. The implementation must count live, queued, and externally requested infantry together, request through only an enabled/free/buildable queue, pause new spending while the player has zero construction-yard/MCV, refinery, or harvester assets, and give already-active transport, protection, repair, and urgent crate assignments precedence while reserving new infantry before ordinary exploration and general squads.

## Plan

Add a VIKI-only `EarlyInfantryRushBotModule` with a small world-independent policy and production/formation tests. During the first 9,000 ticks it will interleave bounded external requests toward twenty total `e2` and four total `e5`, without duplicate commitments or a busy/unbuildable queue request. Four chemical warriors is the bounded interpretation of "individual chemical troopers": it is enough to visibly split across the three requested economy roles without turning the opening into an unbounded infantry drain. The initial 7,500-tick window was raised after the ordinary Empire Earth cycle showed that a normal VIKI only reached eighteen grenadiers before that boundary while sharing its infantry queue with the authored opening; 9,000 ticks leaves time for the literal two groups without extending into the midgame.

Claim deterministic eligible actors each tick before ordinary squad assignment. Launch exactly two lifetime grenadier groups only when ten members are assembled; assign individual chemical missions only when a distinct visible, attackable, reachable economy target exists. Give each grenadier a unique reachable formation cell with two-cell spacing on the near side of a target, issue attacks only from the configured five-cell ring, reform away from visible armed mobile enemies, and stop for 75 ticks after a target dies before selecting or advancing to the next one. Persist request ownership, group membership/targets/holds, individual missions, retired chemical actors, and lifetime group count. Log only meaningful request, assignment, target, reform, hold, completion, and release transitions behind a default-off switch.

Static verification will cover production ratios/caps, exact group launch, target scoring, two-cell formation selection, hold timing, build and YAML validation. Full-engine cycles will first use an instrumented literal map, then ordinary VIKI matches. Adversarial cycles will force (1) queue pressure plus missing critical economy, (2) exploration/transport/protection contention and partial/dead groups, and (3) constrained geometry, nearby defenders, target death splash delay, and save/load. The final regression will repeat the literal scenario with all ordinary modules enabled, followed by a natural fastest match.

Design result: one VIKI-only persisted specialist manager owns bounded early requests, at most two lifetime grenadier launches, and at most four lifetime chemical missions. It reserves provisional actors ahead of ordinary exploration, respects already-active crate/transport/protection/resupply claims, uses deterministic target and formation ordering, reforms away from mobile armed threats, and retains lifetime caps across branch loss and save/load. No balance, weapon, unit, or structure definition changed. Remaining risk is intentionally tactical: a group may be heavily depleted by a defended rush, and chemical missions release when the unit or target ceases to be valid rather than forcing an unsafe stale order.
