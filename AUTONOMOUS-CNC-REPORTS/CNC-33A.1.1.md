## Literal acceptance

In ordinary full-engine games, SkyNet, VIKI, Iron Reaper, and Brutalis follow deterministic compatible opening goals without serializing unrelated idle construction or unit queues. VIKI and Iron Reaper use SkyNet's core structure order but omit a helipad from the ordered opening. Brutalis uses the same core order but completes a second airfield, inserting enough power to keep construction viable, before its first radar. Faction-compatible alternatives satisfy the same semantic goal rather than stalling on an unavailable actor type. Smart economy decides whether refinery work is needed and serializes that work, but it does not select non-refinery structures ahead of the preferred opening or the original configured chance/fraction-and-limit planner.

Each hard AI rapidly reaches five total live-plus-queued harvesters and then requests exactly one opening MCV. Losses before completion are replaced toward the goal without duplicate MCV requests. After those milestones, ordinary configured construction, production, economy recovery, and idle queues take over. The literal starvation regression starts VIKI with scarce cash and observes that it cannot buy three barracks while remaining at only one harvester.

The matchup gate is also player-visible. VIKI must reliably construct an HQ, tech into available covert/stealth production, form the configured stealth-tank harassment groups, and suppress or eliminate exposed enemy harvesters. Harassment scoring should strongly prefer nearer safe prey; when no safe target is immediately available, groups should wait near reachable Tiberium fields where harvesters are likely to return. Fixed hunting-ground partitions are explicitly excluded. Movement and engagement should avoid parking or routing over blue Tiberium, especially during active explosions, and avoid crossing ordinary firefights that catch the squad in crossfire. Crushing lone guarding infantry, kiting tanks from beyond their return range, and hit-and-run harvester attacks are intended safe patterns. If every useful target remains defended for a sustained interval, the squad should stop waiting indefinitely and cautiously clear the weakest viable defender first, taking heavy inspiration from the air-squad safety and defender-selection model while accounting for stealth tanks' different ground threats and targets. At long distance against two Brutalis opponents lacking adequate detection, VIKI should exploit stealth and decisively dominate. Iron Reaper's opening helipad omission is opening-only: against covert/VIKI it must later build helipads and effective aircraft using SkyNet's proven matchup response. SkyNet must scale beyond one helipad when its air-fleet production, rearm, and repair demand requires it, and a helipad that is producing aircraft must still accept concurrent aircraft repair so the army does not wait behind one facility. The benchmark is the repository AI from before autonomous Codex changes: updated bots must consistently outperform that random planner, and a control loss triggers diagnosis of the policy assumptions rather than being dismissed as variance.

## Result and validation

The first iteration preserves the original configured chance/fraction selector for every non-refinery structure, confines smart-economy structure selection to refinery work, adds capability-aware deterministic openings, serializes the five-harvester/one-MCV milestones, restores Iron Reaper air demand and SkyNet pad scaling/repair independence, and makes stealth routing distinguish detectors/active crossfire from harmless idle weapons while avoiding blue-resource hazards. VIKI now uses SkyNet's stealth-tank weight and a shorter signature-unit production delay; this is a config-level preference within the retained random unit selector.

Strict Debug build and interface checks pass with zero warnings/errors. All 385 unit tests pass, including 31 focused stealth-policy tests. Lua syntax checks and exhaustive CNC MiniYAML, sequences, and map validation pass. Debug instrumentation remains opt-in through the existing debug switches and control-map overrides.

The task is capped as `first iteration`, not `complete`: the required allied long-distance VIKI-versus-two-Brutalis domination did not pass, the updated bot did not consistently outperform the legacy control on outcome, and the isolated save reload fixture was invalid. The safe improvements and full evidence are publishable, but a follow-up must address VIKI's loss of its base/economy under two-player pressure rather than further relaxing stealth route safety, then stage the external map before rerunning save/load.
