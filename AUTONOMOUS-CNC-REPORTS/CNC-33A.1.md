## Literal acceptance

In a full-engine ordinary VIKI match, first measure the feature-disabled/normal VIKI's actual construction-credit split rather than inferring a percentage from authored building fractions. After an AI owns at least one unloading refinery, if useful war-factory or airfield capacity is inadequate and a viable production-building candidate is buildable, refinery work from opening, minimum, smart-throughput, excess-cash, or authored-fraction paths combined may occupy at most half of its active Fact construction slots. The remaining eligible capacity must establish useful vehicle production early and keep converting cash into combat units and harvesters. Literal-zero unloading-refinery recovery may serialize one Fact and briefly reserve scarce cash until that refinery is live.

Refineries remain uncapped, because they are cheap to simulate and reduce harvester travel. Direct and refinery-granted harvesters retain the shared 90 live-plus-queued target, with a small incidental overshoot acceptable. Sustained floating cash is a failure when useful production is available, but zero credits at every sampled instant is not required. Missing a critical Fact/MCV, unloading refinery, or harvester means literally owning zero of that class.

The player-visible gate is earlier useful vehicle capacity, occupied production queues, and at least comparable early survival/economy in matched ordinary-map VIKI games, plus materially better scaling/outcome on small low-cash maps with limited nearby Tiberium. Evidence must report refinery versus production-building starts and credits, time to useful vehicle capacity, Fact and unit-queue occupancy, spending, harvester growth, income, army/assets, and match outcome.

Forbidden outcomes are a refinery count cap; more than half of active Facts committed to refineries after one unloading refinery exists while a useful vehicle-production candidate is viable; treating `proc: 31` or other per-type ceilings as a measured 31% budget; idling Facts or cash when vehicle production is blocked/unavailable/already adequate; exceeding the shared harvester target through direct/free production; feature-only weakening of the control; or regressing opening behavior, 10K many-production recovery, literal-zero recovery, save/load, SkyNet, replay determinism, or release log quietness.

## Implementation

- `SmartEconomyEarlyVehicleFactoryPercent` owns the configurable construction share (50 by default). The manager counts distinct live Fact actors, viable vehicle-factory types, and live/queued/reserved factories, then reserves deterministic war-factory/airfield work before any noncritical refinery source when capacity is below the active-Fact floor.
- The same percentage defines the long-run construction balance. At 50%, every committed refinery raises the desired live/queued/reserved vehicle-factory count by one; this alternates new capacity on a single Fact and preserves half the simultaneous Facts when several are available. Refineries remain uncapped, authored limits and buildability still apply, and blocked/unavailable vehicle capacity falls through to ordinary construction.
- Literal-zero refinery recovery remains the only global cash reservation. Sustained throughput may start one streaming refinery with low cash when no refinery is already queued/reserved; additional parallel refineries require their uncommitted cost up front. This prevents active unit queues from consuming every income pulse forever without pausing those queues.
- Vehicle-factory reservations persist through saves and release on queue transition, completion, expiry, Fact loss, or invalid ownership. A debug-only production-spend ledger covers building, random/adaptive unit, and external-request starts. All release YAML switches remain false; custom evidence maps opt in explicitly.

## Audit findings

- The configurable parallel opening added by `41c94a525d` is enabled for Skynet and Iron Reaper, not VIKI. It advances independent authored structure goals; it does not define a normalized combat/economy construction budget.
- VIKI's `proc: 31`, `weap: 36`, and similar `BuildingFractions` are shuffled per-type population ceilings, not spending shares. The observed near-50/50 refinery/factory spend is emergent and therefore needs an explicit construction-capacity invariant for smart VIKI.
- `EconomyCombatSplit: 0.5` belongs to weighted mobile-unit selection. VIKI does not enable `WeightedUnitSelection` or define `EconomyTypes`, so applying that 0.5 directly to VIKI would silently change its normal unit-production personality.
- CNC-33A's `EffectiveParallelRefineryLimit` counts only idle-plus-pending refinery-capable queues and rounds half upward. It therefore does not represent all active Fact slots, allows two refinery commitments out of three Facts, and gates refinery requests without first assigning the preserved capacity to vehicle factories. Opening, minimum, smart-throughput, and authored-fraction refineries do share the reservation gate, so the smallest fix is to correct that central gate and add a vehicle-factory reservation/priority before those paths.
